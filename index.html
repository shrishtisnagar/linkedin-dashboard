📖 How to Export Your Analytics

Follow these steps to export your LinkedIn analytics data and upload it to this dashboard.

🏢

Company Page Analytics

For LinkedIn company pages

📋 YOU'LL NEED TO EXPORT:
• Content analytics (required) — contains post performance data
• Followers analytics (optional) — contains follower demographics
1
Go to your Company Page on LinkedIn
2
Click Analytics in the left menu
3
Select the Content tab
You should see a graph with your content performance
4
Choose your timeframe and click Export in the upper-right corner
We recommend "Past 28 days" or a custom past month
5
Repeat for the Followers tab
Important: Use the exact same timeframe as step 4
6
Download both XLS files and upload them here
The files will be accessible in your Downloads folder
👤

Personal Profile Analytics

For individual LinkedIn profiles

✨ EASIER THAN COMPANY PAGES
Personal profiles export all data in one file — no matter which tab you're on, you'll get the same comprehensive export with all your analytics data.
1
Go to your LinkedIn profile
2
Scroll down to the Analytics section
3
Click Show all analytics →
4
Click any tab (Posts, Audience, etc.)
It doesn't matter which — they all export the same file
5
Choose your timeframe and click Export
We recommend "Past 28 days" or a custom past month
6
Download the XLS file and upload it here
One file contains everything you need
💡 Pro Tips
• Consistent timeframes: Use the same date range for all exports to ensure accurate comparisons
• Monthly cadence: Export at the same time each month to track trends over time
• File naming: The tool will organize everything by client name and month automatically
